Never assume that the vehicle specs that someone gives you are correct, and never assume that you remember a machine's exact specs correctly. I forgot to take a picture of a VIN tag on a Silverado, asked the customer if it was a 2500 (he said yes), and ordered all the right brake parts...except it was a 1500, which has different axles and therefore different brakes.
